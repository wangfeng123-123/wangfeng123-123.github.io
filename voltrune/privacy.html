Voltrune – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This policy applies to the Android game Voltrune (package com.voltrune.game).

Summary

Voltrune does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. The game works fully offline. The app declares the Android internet permission, but the game does not make any network connections; it requests no other Android permission.

Data the game keeps on your device

Your progress (stars, crystals, unlocked ships, upgrades, records) and your settings (sound, music, language) are saved only on your device, inside the app's private storage. This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time with "Erase progress" in the settings, by clearing the app's data in Android settings, or by uninstalling the game.

What the game does not do

  • No account, sign-in or registration.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party SDKs.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • No network connections of any kind.

Children

Voltrune is not directed at children under 13. Since the game collects no data at all, it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
