Sparkstone – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy applies to the Android game Sparkstone.

Summary

Sparkstone does not sell personal data. The game can be played offline. The app connects to our server to check for and load updated content for the game pages. To understand where installs come from and how the app is used, it uses the analytics services AppsFlyer and Adjust, which collect limited device data described below under “Analytics and attribution”.

Internet access

Network activity consists of checking for and loading updated content for the game pages from our server, plus the analytics traffic described below. The content request sends no information about you or your device beyond what any web request necessarily involves (such as your IP address, which the hosting provider may record in standard server logs).

Data stored on your device

Your progress, scores, records and settings are saved only on your device, inside the app's private storage. This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time with the reset option inside the app, by clearing the app's data in Android settings, or by uninstalling the game.

What the game does not do

  • No account, sign-in or registration.
  • No advertising shown in the game.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Analytics and attribution

We use AppsFlyer (AppsFlyer Ltd.) and Adjust (Adjust GmbH) to understand where installs come from and how the app is used. These services may collect your Google advertising ID, device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, language and app version), IP address, install referrer, and in-app events (such as opening the app or signing in to our online content). We do not send them your name, user ID, transaction amounts or any other personal details. They process this data on our behalf under their own privacy policies: AppsFlyer and Adjust. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads. We do not use this data to show you advertising.

Children

Sparkstone is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The analytics data described above is not used to identify anyone.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
