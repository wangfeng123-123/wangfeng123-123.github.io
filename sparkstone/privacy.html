Sparkstone – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy applies to the Android game Sparkstone.

Summary

Sparkstone does not collect, sell or share any personal data. The game can be played offline. The app connects to our server to check for and load updated content for the game pages; apart from that it makes no other network connections and requests no other Android permissions.

Internet access

The only network activity is checking for and loading updated content for the game pages from our server. This request sends no information about you or your device beyond what any web request necessarily involves (such as your IP address, which the hosting provider may record in standard server logs). It is not used for advertising, analytics or tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your progress, scores, records and settings are saved only on your device, inside the app's private storage. This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time with the reset option inside the app, by clearing the app's data in Android settings, or by uninstalling the game.

What the game does not do

  • No account, sign-in or registration.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party SDKs.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • No network connections other than loading game content updates from our server.

Children

Sparkstone is not directed at children under 13. Since the game collects no personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
