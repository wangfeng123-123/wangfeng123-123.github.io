Bloco da Sorte – Privacy Policy

Versão em português

Effective date: October 30, 2026

Bloco da Sorte ("the game") is a single-player block puzzle game that runs entirely on your device and works offline.

Information we collect

None. The game does not collect, store on any server, transmit or share any personal information or usage data.

Internet access

The app declares the Internet permission because the system component that displays the game requires it, but the game does not make any network connection. It does not request any other device permission.

Data stored on your device

Your progress, best scores, coins, statistics and settings are saved locally on your device so you can keep playing later. This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time from the game's settings, by clearing the app data or by uninstalling the game.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

The game contains no advertising, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no third-party SDKs.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
