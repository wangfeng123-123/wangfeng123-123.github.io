Bloco da Sorte – Privacy Policy

Versi Bahasa Indonesia

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Bloco da Sorte ("the game") is a single-player block puzzle game that can be played offline.

Information we collect

We do not collect, sell or share any personal information. The game does not collect your personal data or usage data.

Internet access

The game can be played fully without an internet connection. When opened with a connection, it connects to our server only to check for and load updated game page content. There are no other network connections, and no personal data is sent. The game does not request any other device permission.

Data stored on your device

Your progress, best scores, coins, statistics and settings are saved locally on your device so you can keep playing later. This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time from the game's settings, by clearing the app data or by uninstalling the game.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

The game contains no advertising, no analytics, no user accounts, no in-app purchases and no third-party SDKs.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Since the game collects no personal data from anyone, there is nothing to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
