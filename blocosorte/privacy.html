Bloco da Sorte – Privacy Policy

Versi Bahasa Indonesia

Effective date: November 21, 2026

Bloco da Sorte ("the game") is a single-player block puzzle game that can be played offline.

Information we collect

We do not sell personal information. The game itself does not ask for your name, e-mail or any account. To understand where installs come from and how the app is used, it uses the analytics services AppsFlyer and Adjust, which collect limited device data described under “Analytics and attribution”.

Internet access

The game can be played fully without an internet connection. When opened with a connection, it connects to our server to check for and load updated game page content, and the analytics services described below send their data. The content request involves only what any web request necessarily involves (such as your IP address, which the hosting provider may record in standard server logs).

Data stored on your device

Your progress, best scores, coins, statistics and settings are saved locally on your device so you can keep playing later. This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time from the game's settings, by clearing the app data or by uninstalling the game.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

The game shows no advertising and has no user accounts or in-app purchases.

Analytics and attribution

We use AppsFlyer (AppsFlyer Ltd.) and Adjust (Adjust GmbH) to understand where installs come from and how the app is used. These services may collect your Google advertising ID, device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, language and app version), IP address, install referrer, and in-app events (such as opening the app or signing in to our online content). We do not send them your name, user ID, transaction amounts or any other personal details. They process this data on our behalf under their own privacy policies: AppsFlyer and Adjust. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads. We do not use this data to show you advertising.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The analytics data described above is not used to identify anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
