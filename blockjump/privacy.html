Privacy Policy – Block Jump

Versión en español

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy explains how the Block Jump mobile app ("the app") handles information.

Information the app collects

The app does not ask for, collect or transmit personal information. It has no account, no sign-up, no advertising and no in-app purchases.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device only, so that it is there the next time you play:

  • your settings (sound, music, vibration, language and chosen skin);
  • your game statistics (best score, games played, jumps, combos), your gems, skins and daily missions.

This information never leaves your device. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The app requests the internet permission. It is used for one purpose only: to check for and download updates to the game itself. This request sends no personal information and nothing from your device; as with any internet request, the hosting provider sees your IP address and may record it in standard server logs. The game is stored inside the app and works without an internet connection. The app requests no other permissions.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects how information is handled, this policy will be updated and the date above will change.
